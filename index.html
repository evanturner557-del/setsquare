Which trades is this for?

Heating and plumbing firms with 2–20 people first. Electrical, building, roofing, landscaping and similar firms run a similar office, so the procedures also fit with light editing.

No. Setsquare covers office work — enquiries, quotes, booking, sign-off, invoicing, complaints and new starters. Safety procedures, risk assessments and trade regulations are separate and stay your responsibility.

Both. You get editable documents in either format, plus printable one-page checklists.

Can I change the procedures?

Yes — they're yours to edit and use in your own business. Please don't resell or share them outside your firm.
